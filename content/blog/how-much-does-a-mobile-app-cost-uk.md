The honest answer is that it depends on what the app does. A simple app with a handful of screens costs a fraction of an app with accounts, payments and a custom backend. Here is what moves the price, what other guides say, and what we charge.

## What drives the price

- **Screens and features.** More screens and more behaviour mean more build and testing time. This is the biggest factor.
- **Accounts, payments and notifications.** Each one adds integration work and testing.
- **A backend.** If the app stores data or has user accounts, something has to run behind it. A backend typically adds {{addon:extra_backend}} to a project.
- **Design.** If you already have designs, the build is quicker. If you only have sketches, basic interface design adds {{addon:extra_design}}.
- **Platforms.** Android only is the simplest. Supporting iOS as well adds work and is scoped per project.
- **Release.** Preparing the store listing and release takes time, and developer accounts have their own fees.

## What other guides say

Published UK guides mostly describe agency builds. puts a basic MVP at £20,000 to £60,000 over 8 to 16 weeks, and UK freelancer rates at £45 to £90 per hour. gives £8,000 to £30,000 over 8 to 12 weeks for a simple app. Treat these as rough guides. They are written by agencies, and "simple" and "MVP" mean different things to different people.

## Ways to keep the cost down

- Start with one platform and add the second later.
- Keep the first version to what your users actually need.
- Bring sketches or designs, even rough ones.
- Use a ready-made service such as Firebase instead of a custom backend where it fits.
- Agree a small first milestone and review it before the next one.
